About MBPS

Property sourcing built around the investor.

100%
Off-market access

Moyo Bespoke Property Solutions exists for one purpose: to make UK property investment feel effortless — without sacrificing returns or sleep.

Every brief starts with a private consultation. We profile your goals, capital position, risk appetite and timeline, then go to market with a brief no estate agent will ever see. Most of our deals close before they ever appear on Rightmove.

We're a team — a dedicated client-support function backed by a panel of independent solicitors, mortgage and finance brokers, accountants and trusted contractors. That means whatever your deal needs, the right specialist is already on hand. We work across single-let Buy-to-Let, professional HMO, Serviced Accommodation, commercial conversions and rent-to-rent agreements.

What We Do

Five investment strategies,
one curated experience.

Every strategy we support is one we know intimately — from underwriting and refurbishment costs through to tenant management and exit. We won't source what we wouldn't buy ourselves.

01

Buy-to-Let (BTL)

Single-let acquisitions selected for strong rental coverage, capital growth potential and minimal management overhead. The foundation of every balanced UK portfolio.

Typical entry£100k – £400k
Target gross yield6% – 9%
02

Houses in Multiple Occupation (HMO)

Room-by-room let to professionals or students. Higher gross yields, more intensive management — best suited to investors comfortable with operations or willing to fund a managed solution.

Typical entry£180k – £500k
Target gross yield10% – 15%
03

Serviced Accommodation (SA)

Short-stay lettings in business and leisure-led locations. Strong cash flow when location, finish and channel management align. We model occupancy and ANR before you commit a penny.

Typical entry£150k – £400k
Target gross yield12% – 20%
04

Commercial & Mixed-Use

Commercial-to-residential conversions, mixed-use stock and small block opportunities. Higher complexity, materially better returns — for investors with patience and finance in place.

Typical entry£250k+
Target gross yield10% – 18%
05

Rent to Rent (R2R)

Lease from a landlord, operate the property as a serviced or multi-let asset, and keep the spread. Lower capital outlay, faster cashflow — the right entry strategy for investors building their first £500–£1,000 per month before they commit purchase capital. We source compliant landlord agreements, model the operating numbers, and structure the company let so both sides are protected.

Typical setup£3k – £8k
Target monthly net£500 – £1,500
Best forHMO & SA models
No mortgage required✓ Yes
How We Work

A clear path, from brief to completion.

Sourcing is part craft, part discipline. Here's exactly how we engage from the first introduction.

01

Discovery Call

A 30-minute conversation to understand your goals, capital, risk appetite and timeline. No fee, no pressure.

02

Onboarding

Sign the Deal Sourcing Agreement and pay the engagement deposit. Your investor profile is locked in writing.

03

Sourcing

We work to your brief — off-market, direct-to-vendor and curated agent relationships. You see only the deals that fit.

04

Deal Pack

Each opportunity arrives with full financial modelling, comparable analysis, refurbishment costing and exit options.

05

Completion

We co-ordinate solicitors, broker, surveyor and contractors through to keys-in-hand — and stay on as long as you need.
